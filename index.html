TFW Program Violations Tracker

A searchable record of employers that the Government of Canada has officially found non-compliant under the Temporary Foreign Worker Program (TFWP) or International Mobility Program (IMP).

Database About & context

What this list is: every row below reflects a final compliance decision published by the Government of Canada — the employer was inspected and found to have violated one or more conditions of hiring a temporary foreign worker. It does not include employers who simply applied for or received a Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA); being approved for an LMIA is not evidence of wrongdoing on its own. See About & context for sourcing, licensing, and the limits of this data.

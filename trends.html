Approved positions over time

Total worker positions approved per calendar year (the underlying data is released quarterly, grouped here by year since that's the more useful view), color-coded by the 8 occupations with the most approved positions overall, with everything else grouped into "Other" (still roughly 40% of the total — the occupation list is long-tailed across 569 distinct titles). Click any segment of a bar to drill in: an "Other" segment opens the list of occupations it contains; any other segment goes straight to the list of employers, ranked by positions and filterable by province. The government's 2014–2015 releases didn't list occupations at all, so those two years aren't shown here (they're still in the LMIA Database). 2026 includes only Q1 so far — don't read it as a drop from 2025.

Approved LMIAs by year, by top occupation

Number of approved LMIA applications per calendar year (not worker positions — a single LMIA can cover multiple positions). Color-coded by the 8 occupations with the most approved LMIAs overall, with every other occupation grouped into "Other" (still the majority here — LMIA counts are spread across more occupations than positions are). Click any segment to drill in — "Other" opens the occupation list, any other segment goes straight to the employer list, ranked by approved LMIAs and filterable by province. The government's 2014–2015 releases didn't list occupations at all, so those two years aren't shown. 2026 includes only Q1.

Top franchise brands

Locations identified either by an explicit "o/a" / "d.b.a." / "c/o" marker (e.g. "1234 Alberta Ltd. o/a Tim Hortons"), or — for brands with enough of those explicit matches to trust — by the brand name appearing anywhere in the business name (e.g. plain "Tim Hortons", "989240 Alberta Ltd Tim Hortons"). It still won't catch every franchisee (a location named only "Smith Holdings Inc." with nothing indicating the brand isn't detectable from the name alone), so treat this as a lower bound, not a full franchise census — it will not add up to the "Top occupations" chart below. That chart counts every position for an occupation regardless of employer (independent restaurants, hotels, institutional kitchens, food-processing plants, farm camp cooks, etc.), while this one only counts the small number of recognizable named chains. E.g. the food-brand bars here sum to roughly 6,500 positions, versus ~55,000 for "Cooks" alone below — most food-sector hiring isn't at a detectable franchise.
